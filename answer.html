Help is on the Way!

A disaster response team has been assigned to your location. Please remain in a safe and accessible place and follow emergency instructions.

1. Drop, Cover, and Hold On: Drop to your hands and knees, take cover under a sturdy desk or table, and hold on until the shaking stops.
2. Stay Away from Glass and Heavy Objects: Move away from windows, mirrors, bookshelves, and light fixtures that could shatter or fall.
3. Stay Indoors Until Shaking Stops: Do not run outside during the shaking, as falling debris near building exits poses a high risk.
4. Move to an Open Area if Outdoors: If you are already outside, move to a clear spot away from buildings, trees, streetlights, and utility wires.
5. Expect Aftershocks: Be ready to drop, cover, and hold on again when secondary tremors occur after the main quake.
